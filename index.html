Overview: This Privacy Policy describes how Dekho Aur Jano ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, handles, and protects your information when you use our Android mobile application. We are committed to maintaining user trust, transparency, and compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies.

1. Permissions & Data Accessed

Dekho Aur Jano is designed with privacy and data minimization in mind. The app requests only the permissions strictly necessary to deliver its core camera scanning, audio dialogue, and smart AI features:

Permission Purpose Data Retention / Sharing
android.permission.CAMERA Allows taking photos or capturing live visual frames to scan text, medicines, documents, nutrition labels, and objects. Camera imagery is processed in memory for visual recognition and is never sold or stored permanently on any third-party marketing servers.
android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO Enables hands-free voice dialogue and spoken queries in Indian regional languages (Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, English). Audio input is converted to speech queries transiently during the session. We do not maintain perpetual voice recordings.
android.permission.INTERNET Required to communicate with secure Google Gemini APIs for OCR, translation, AI explanations, and Google AdMob ad networks. Standard HTTPS encrypted transmission over TLS/SSL.

Zero Broad Storage Permissions: In compliance with Google Play storage policies, our app does not request broad storage access (such as READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE). User photo selection utilizes Android's secure system Photo Picker.

2. Third-Party Services & SDKs

Our application integrates trusted third-party service providers who may collect information used to identify your device in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Used to deliver Banner, Interstitial, and Rewarded advertisements. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID / GAID), approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction telemetry.
    Read Google's Advertising Privacy & Terms and Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google AI & Gemini API Services: Used to analyze text, images, and generate multi-turn explanations and translations. Requests are routed over encrypted endpoints.

3. In-App Studio Creations & Local Storage

Any drafts, prompts, or generated creations in the AI Studio are stored locally on your device or kept transiently during your active user session. You have full control to:

  • Edit or update prompt text at any time.
  • Delete individual creation items directly from the Recent Creations screen.
  • Clear all creation history with a single tap.

4. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly take steps to delete any such data.

No transmission over the Internet or electronic storage method is 100% secure, but we continually adopt modern Android security standards and least-privilege principles.
